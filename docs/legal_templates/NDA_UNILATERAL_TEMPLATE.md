## 1. Purpose

The Disclosing Party wishes to disclose certain confidential information to the Receiving Party in connection with `[describe purpose: e.g. evaluation of an investment opportunity / employment negotiation / professional services consultation]` (the "**Purpose**"). This Agreement sets out the terms under which such disclosure occurs.

The Receiving Party is not expected to disclose any confidential information of its own. To the extent the Receiving Party voluntarily discloses any confidential information, this Agreement shall not protect it; the Parties shall execute a separate mutual NDA if mutual protection is desired.

---

## 2. Definitions

**2.1** "**Confidential Information**" means any non-public information disclosed by the Disclosing Party to the Receiving Party in connection with the Purpose, whether disclosed orally, in writing, electronically, by visual inspection, or in any other form, including without limitation:

(a) technical information (architectures, source code, algorithms, configurations, audit-locked invariants, performance data);
(b) commercial information (pricing models, customer lists, prospect pipelines, financial statements, revenue, projections);
(c) operational information (staff, processes, vendors, deployment details);
(d) intellectual property (patent applications, defensive disclosures, trade secrets, trademarks-in-progress);
(e) any information marked "Confidential" or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the circumstances of disclosure.

**2.2** "**Permitted Recipients**" means the Receiving Party's directors, officers, employees, professional advisors (lawyers, accountants), and contractors who: (a) have a legitimate need to access the Confidential Information for the Purpose; (b) have been informed of the confidential nature; and (c) are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than those in this Agreement.

**2.3** "**Effective Date**" means the date this Agreement is executed by both Parties.

---

## 3. Confidentiality obligations

**3.1** The Receiving Party shall:

(a) hold the Confidential Information in strict confidence;
(b) use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose;
(c) not disclose the Confidential Information to any third party other than Permitted Recipients;
(d) protect the Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of similar sensitivity, and in no event less than reasonable care;
(e) not copy, modify, reverse-engineer, or create derivative works from the Confidential Information except as strictly necessary for the Purpose;
(f) immediately notify the Disclosing Party in writing upon discovering any unauthorized disclosure or use.

**3.2** The Receiving Party is responsible for ensuring its Permitted Recipients comply with this Agreement and shall be liable for any breach by any Permitted Recipient as if such breach were its own.

**3.3** **No use against the Disclosing Party.** The Receiving Party shall not use the Confidential Information to: (a) compete with the Disclosing Party; (b) reverse-engineer the Disclosing Party's products or services; (c) solicit the Disclosing Party's customers, employees, or contractors; or (d) develop substantially similar products or services.

---

## 4. Carve-outs

**4.1** The obligations in Section 3 do not apply to information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate through written records:

(a) was already known to the Receiving Party without restriction prior to disclosure;
(b) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party or its Permitted Recipients;
(c) was independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information;
(d) was lawfully received from a third party without restriction.

**4.2** **Required disclosure.** If the Receiving Party is required by law, court order, or regulatory authority to disclose Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall: (a) where legally permitted, provide prompt written notice to the Disclosing Party; (b) disclose only the minimum necessary to comply; (c) cooperate with the Disclosing Party in seeking confidential treatment.

## 5. Term

**5.2** **Survival.** The confidentiality obligations in Section 3 shall continue for `[5]` years from the date of disclosure of each item of Confidential Information, notwithstanding termination or expiry of this Agreement.

## 6. Return or destruction

**6.1** Upon termination of this Agreement, or upon written request from the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall within 30 days return all materials containing Confidential Information or destroy such materials and certify destruction in writing, at the Disclosing Party's election.

**6.2** **Backup retention exception.** The Receiving Party may retain Confidential Information solely to the extent: (a) required by law; or (b) automatically retained in routine archived backups, provided that any retained information continues to be subject to this Agreement until destruction.

## 10. Notices

**10.1** Any notice or communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the address set out at the head of this Agreement (or such other address as either Party may notify the other in writing). Notice shall be deemed served:

(a) if delivered in person, on delivery;

provided that any notice received outside Business Hours shall be deemed served on the next Business Day. "**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday, or public holiday in the Republic of Kenya.

## Drafter's commentary (REMOVE BEFORE EXECUTION)

### When unilateral is wrong
This template is for asymmetric disclosure. If the receiving party will reveal anything substantive in return (architecture details from their side, financial particulars, etc.), use `NDA_MUTUAL_TEMPLATE.md` instead.

### Investor-pitch use
Many VCs refuse mutual NDAs because they evaluate hundreds of pitches; mutual obligations would compound. Unilateral pitches are increasingly common but some investors still resist any NDA. If an investor refuses this template entirely, A2Z faces a choice: disclose less (relying on the public github content as the sales pitch) or proceed without NDA.

### Section 3.3 non-use clause
This is stricter than typical mutual NDAs. Worth keeping for investor and contractor unilateral contexts because the asymmetric disclosure creates a stronger competitive risk. Counterparties may push back on (a) and (d) — those are the most aggressive provisions. (b) and (c) are standard.

### Section 7.2 IP attribution
Explicitly references audit-locked patterns + 9-gate perimeter + Living Documentation system to put the Receiving Party on notice that these are claimed as A2Z's IP. Useful for downstream patent prosecution and trade-secret enforcement.

### Survival period asymmetry
5 years is conservative for unilateral disclosure given the asymmetry. A receiving party may push back to 3 years; that's negotiable. Lawyer to advise on Kenyan-statutory limits if any.

### What's not included
- **Non-solicitation** — separate document if needed
- **Liability cap** — not standard for unilateral NDAs; full damages available to the Disclosing Party
- **Press release / publicity** — handled by separate agreement if applicable
