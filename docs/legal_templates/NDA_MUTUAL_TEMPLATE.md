## 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to engage in discussions concerning `[describe purpose: e.g. potential pilot deployment of A2Z MIS 360 banking management intelligence platform / potential contractor engagement / potential commercial partnership]` (the "**Purpose**"). In connection with the Purpose, each Party may disclose to the other certain Confidential Information.

This Agreement sets out the terms under which Confidential Information may be exchanged.

---

## 2. Definitions

**2.1** "**Confidential Information**" means any information disclosed by one Party (the "**Disclosing Party**") to the other Party (the "**Receiving Party**") in connection with the Purpose, whether disclosed orally, in writing, electronically, by visual inspection, or in any other form, including without limitation:

(a) technical information (architectures, source code, algorithms, configurations, performance data);
(b) commercial information (pricing, customer lists, prospect pipelines, financial data, business plans, revenue);
(c) operational information (staff, processes, vendors, deployment details);
(d) personal data within the meaning of the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019;
(e) any information marked "Confidential" or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the circumstances of disclosure.

**2.2** "**Permitted Recipients**" means each Party's directors, officers, employees, professional advisors (including lawyers and accountants), and contractors who: (a) have a legitimate need to access the Confidential Information for the Purpose; (b) have been informed of the confidential nature of the information; and (c) are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than those in this Agreement.

**2.3** "**Effective Date**" means the date this Agreement is executed by both Parties.

---

## 3. Confidentiality obligations

**3.1** Each Receiving Party shall:

(a) hold the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information in strict confidence;
(b) use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose;
(c) not disclose the Confidential Information to any third party other than Permitted Recipients;
(d) protect the Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of similar sensitivity, and in no event less than reasonable care;
(e) not copy, modify, reverse-engineer, or create derivative works from the Confidential Information except as necessary for the Purpose;
(f) immediately notify the Disclosing Party in writing upon discovering any unauthorized disclosure or use of Confidential Information.

**3.2** Each Receiving Party is responsible for ensuring its Permitted Recipients comply with this Agreement and shall be liable for any breach by any Permitted Recipient as if such breach were its own.

---

## 4. Carve-outs

**4.1** The obligations in Section 3 do not apply to information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate through written records:

(a) was already known to the Receiving Party without restriction prior to disclosure by the Disclosing Party;
(b) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party or its Permitted Recipients;
(c) was independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information;
(d) was lawfully received from a third party without restriction.

**4.2** **Required disclosure.** If the Receiving Party is required by law, court order, or regulatory authority (including the Central Bank of Kenya, the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner, or a Kenyan court of competent jurisdiction) to disclose Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall:

(a) where legally permitted, provide prompt written notice to the Disclosing Party so that the Disclosing Party may seek a protective order;
(b) disclose only the minimum Confidential Information necessary to comply;
(c) cooperate with the Disclosing Party in seeking confidential treatment of any disclosed information.

---

## 5. Personal data

**5.1** The Parties acknowledge that Confidential Information may include personal data within the meaning of the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019.

**5.2** Each Party shall:

(a) process such personal data only to the extent necessary for the Purpose;
(b) implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or destruction;
(c) notify the other Party without undue delay (and in any event within 72 hours) of any personal data breach affecting the other Party's data;
(d) cooperate with the other Party in fulfilling data subject rights and regulatory obligations.

**5.3** Where personal-data processing under this Agreement requires a Data Processing Agreement under Section 41 of the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019, the Parties shall execute such an agreement separately.

---

## 6. Term

**6.1** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for `[2]` years thereafter, unless earlier terminated by either Party giving 30 days' written notice ("**Term**").

**6.2** **Survival of confidentiality obligations.** Notwithstanding termination or expiry of this Agreement, the confidentiality obligations in Section 3 shall continue for `[5]` years from the date of disclosure of each item of Confidential Information.

---

## 7. Return or destruction

**7.1** Upon termination of this Agreement, or upon written request from the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall within 30 days:

(a) return all materials containing Confidential Information; or
(b) destroy such materials and certify destruction in writing,

at the Disclosing Party's election.

**7.2** **Backup retention exception.** The Receiving Party may retain Confidential Information solely to the extent: (a) required by law or regulatory requirements; or (b) automatically retained in routine archived backups, provided that any retained information continues to be subject to this Agreement until destruction.

---

## 8. Ownership and no license

**8.1** All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. No license or other rights in respect of any patent, copyright, trademark, trade secret, or other intellectual property right are granted by this Agreement, whether expressly or by implication.

**8.2** Each Party retains all rights in its pre-existing intellectual property. Nothing in this Agreement transfers ownership of any intellectual property between the Parties.

---

## 9. No representation, no warranty

**9.1** All Confidential Information is provided "as is". Neither Party makes any representation or warranty as to the accuracy, completeness, or fitness for purpose of any Confidential Information disclosed.

**9.2** Neither Party has any obligation to disclose any specific Confidential Information; each Party may decide in its sole discretion what (if any) Confidential Information to disclose.

---

## 10. Equitable remedies

**10.1** The Parties acknowledge that monetary damages may be an inadequate remedy for breach of this Agreement and that the Disclosing Party may seek injunctive relief or specific performance, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity, without the requirement to post bond.

---

## 11. Notices

**11.1** Any notice or communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the address set out at the head of this Agreement (or such other address as either Party may notify the other in writing). Notice shall be deemed served:

(a) if delivered in person, on delivery;
(b) if sent by registered post, on the third Business Day after posting;
(c) if sent by email to an address designated by the receiving Party for notices, on receipt of the email's delivery confirmation;

provided that any notice received outside Business Hours shall be deemed served on the next Business Day.

**11.2** "**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday, or public holiday in the Republic of Kenya.

---

## 12. Assignment

**12.1** Neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that either Party may assign this Agreement to a successor in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or substantially all of its assets, provided that the successor agrees in writing to be bound by this Agreement.

---

## 13. Entire agreement; amendment

**13.1** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties concerning its subject matter and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, and agreements, whether written or oral.

**13.2** No amendment or waiver of any provision of this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing signed by both Parties.

---

## 14. Severability

**14.1** If any provision of this Agreement is held by a court of competent jurisdiction to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force, and the invalid or unenforceable provision shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to render it valid and enforceable while preserving the Parties' intent.

## 15. Governing law and jurisdiction

**15.1** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of Kenya.

**15.2** The Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Nairobi, Kenya, in respect of any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, provided that either Party may seek interim or injunctive relief in any court of competent jurisdiction.

**15.3** Notwithstanding clause 15.2, the Parties may by written agreement submit any dispute to arbitration under the Arbitration Act of Kenya 1995, with arbitration seated in Nairobi and conducted in English under the Kenyan Branch of the Chartered Institute of Arbitrators rules.

## Drafter's commentary (REMOVE BEFORE EXECUTION)

> The following notes explain drafting choices. Lawyer to remove or convert to internal commentary before final.

### Why mutual not unilateral
Per the v8.13 IP Strategy Plan Appendix A.2.2: in any serious commercial conversation both sides exchange confidential information. Mutual NDAs are simpler to negotiate (no asymmetry to argue about) and protect both sides.

### 5-year survival period
The plan recommends 3-5 years; 5 is more conservative and protects A2Z as the typically-smaller party with less leverage to enforce. A counterparty may push back to 3 — that's a negotiation point, not a deal-breaker.

### Personal data clause (Section 5)
The Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 requires written DPA for processor relationships. This NDA does not substitute for a DPA but signals that one will be executed when processing scope crystallises. Lawyer to confirm this approach is sufficient for pre-DPA conversations.

### Required-disclosure carve-out (Section 4.2)
Specifically names CBK + ODPC + Kenyan courts since those are the most relevant Kenyan regulators for A2Z's operating context. Lawyer may adjust if foreign regulators are likely (e.g. EU or US bank deployments).

### Equitable remedies (Section 10)
Includes "without requirement to post bond" because Kenyan procedural law sometimes requires bond for injunctive relief; this clause attempts to waive that. Lawyer to confirm enforceability under Kenyan civil procedure.

### Arbitration option (Section 15.3)
Some commercial counterparties prefer arbitration over courts; including the option lets the Parties decide later. If lawyer has strong preference, simplify to one forum.

### Term length
Default 2 years for the agreement itself; survival clause extends confidentiality to 5 years per disclosed item. This is conservative but a common Kenyan-commercial pattern.

### What's not included
- **Non-solicitation clause** — sometimes paired with NDAs but more often a separate agreement; not standard in pure NDA contexts
- **Press release / publicity** restrictions — usually a separate clause in MSAs; could be added if Joshua wants
- **Indemnification** — not standard for mutual NDAs; liability is typically capped at proven damages
- **Insurance requirements** — typical for larger commercial contracts; not for NDAs
