## Drafter's commentary (REMOVE BEFORE EXECUTION)

### Why this matters
Per the v8.13 IP Strategy Plan Appendix A.2.4: in Kenya as in most jurisdictions, IP created by an individual is owned by that individual unless explicitly assigned. If Joshua creates the codebase personally and then incorporates a Ltd, the company does NOT automatically own the IP. Investor due diligence will require this; so will any acquirer; so will any litigation defending the IP.

### Trigger
This template applies ONLY after Joshua incorporates A2Z as a Limited Company in Kenya. While operating as a sole proprietorship, this assignment is not needed (Joshua personally owns everything; the proprietorship is just a trading name).

### Consideration
The standard Kenyan-corporate convention is to assign IP in exchange for shares in the newly-incorporated company. This is what Schedule 1 contemplates. Other consideration mechanisms (cash payment, deferred consideration, royalties) are possible but require more careful tax planning. Lawyer to advise.

### Retroactive effect
The Effective Date clause is set to the company's incorporation date with retroactive effect. This handles the gap between when Joshua started building and when the company was formed. Important for cap-table cleanliness during investor due diligence.

### Future assignment (Section 3)
Section 3 covers IP created AFTER the Effective Date. This is sometimes folded into a separate Employment Agreement; here it's included for belt-and-suspenders coverage. Lawyer to confirm this isn't redundant with planned Employment / Founder Agreement.

### Power of attorney (Section 4.2)
Standard but operationally rarely used. Important for cap-table due diligence — investors want to see that the company has clear paths to perfect IP rights even if the Founder later becomes uncooperative (e.g. departure, dispute).

### Schedule 1 specificity
The description is intentionally specific to current platform state. Specificity reduces ambiguity in litigation; it also signals to investors / acquirers that the cap table is well-organized.

### Witnessing
Kenyan commercial conventions sometimes require witnessing for IP assignments. Lawyer to confirm whether attestation by a Commissioner of Oaths is also needed.

### What's not included
- **Non-compete clause** — usually a separate Founder Agreement; not standard in pure IP Assignment
- **Vesting schedule** — typically governed by the share-issuance documents, not the IP Assignment
- **Restrictive covenants on employment** — separate Employment Agreement scope
